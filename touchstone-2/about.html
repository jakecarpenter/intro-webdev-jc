Our Story

Your local bakery since 1996

Welcome to our neighborhood bakery. We sell breads, pastries, cakes, and more! All made right here, locally, in our town. Our founders recognized the need for a local bakery using local ingredients and have been making delicious breads and other baked goods since then

The Best ingredients

We use only the freshest, highest quality ingredients to make our breads and pastries. We get almost all of them locally.

The Best Staff

Our small but mighty staff is dedicated to making the best baked goods possible. Our bakery manager Jim has been here since the beginning.
